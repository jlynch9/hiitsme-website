The gap no one has filled
Social media gives us a way to collect connections. Messaging apps give us a way to reach them. But nothing gives our connections
depth, meaning, or value.

We’ve built incredible tools for volume — thousands of followers, instant messages, global reach. What we haven’t built is the tool that turns those connections into something real. That remembers why someone matters. That tells you when to reach out. That helps you show up in a way that actually means something.

That’s the gap Hi, It’s Me fills.
Why we built this

We all operate in three circles — professional, social, and family. Each demands something different. All of them suffer when follow-through fails.

In one week, we watched a colleague lose a partnership worth months of work because a follow-up didn’t get sent. A student let three warm referrals go cold. An executive confessed she manages relationships worth millions — with sticky notes.

The tools to send a message exist. The tools to truly know the people you’re sending it to — in every language, at the right moment — didn’t. So we built one.

That became Hi, It’s Me.
