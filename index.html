Why I built this

We all operate in three circles. Our professional circle — the colleagues, clients, and collaborators who shape our careers. Our social circle — the friends, mentors, and kindred spirits who enrich our lives. And our family circle — the people we love most and sometimes reach out to least.

Every one of these circles demands something different. A follow-up in French for a colleague in Casablanca. A birthday reminder for a friend's daughter. A note about your uncle's new business venture. And yet we manage all of it the same way — scattered notes, forgotten promises, and good intentions that never become actions.

I heard the same frustration everywhere. A colleague who lost a major partnership because a follow-up slipped through the cracks. A student with three warm referrals who let all three go cold. An executive who knows her relationships are worth millions — and manages them with sticky notes.

The tools exist to send a message. They don't exist to help you truly understand the people you're sending it to — across every circle, in every language, at the right moment. So I built one.

That became Hi, It's Me.
